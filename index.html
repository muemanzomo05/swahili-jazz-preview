About the Company

Where Tradition
Meets Innovation

Swahili Jazz is a premier Kenyan entertainment company specializing in world-class live music entertainment, event production, artist management, record label services, and entertainment consultancy. We create exceptional experiences for corporate events, festivals, diplomatic missions, hospitality venues, private celebrations, and cultural productions, delivering creativity, professionalism, and excellence at every stage.

Founded by renowned saxophonist, composer, music director, and cultural ambassador Juma Tutu, the company is built on decades of industry experience and trusted partnerships. Juma is a respected and accomplished musician who was conferred the National Hero title by the Government of Kenya in recognition of his outstanding contribution to the nation's music, arts, and cultural heritage.

Guided by our slogan, “The Sounds of the Soul,” Swahili Jazz is committed to connecting people through music, nurturing artistic excellence, and delivering unforgettable entertainment experiences in Kenya and around the world.
