The Studio

Where the Records
Get Made

Swahili Jazz runs its own studio and label. We record our catalogue there, and we open the room to artists working in the same tradition.

Sessions are engineered by people who play. That matters most when you are tracking horns, live drums and a room full of musicians at the same time.

What We Do

Studio and Label Services

  • Recording Tracking for full bands, horn sections and solo instrumentalists.
  • Music production Arrangement and direction, taking an idea through to a finished record.
  • Mixing Balanced mixes that keep the dynamics of a live performance intact.
  • Mastering Final masters prepared for streaming and for physical release.
  • Music distribution Getting releases onto Spotify, Bandcamp and the rest of the platforms.
  • Artist development Working with artists across a body of work rather than a single track.
  • Original productions Commissioned pieces and label releases of our own.
