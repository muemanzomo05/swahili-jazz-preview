How It Works

One Call,
the Whole Line-up

Swahili Jazz books more than its own band. We represent and work alongside musicians across Kenya, so a single brief can cover the entire night.

You get one point of contact, one contract and one team responsible for the music. If an act falls through, replacing them is our problem rather than yours.

What You Can Book

Acts on Our Roster

  • Bands Full line-ups covering jazz, Afro-fusion, benga and popular repertoire.
  • Solo artists Vocalists and instrumentalists for smaller rooms.
  • Saxophonists Solo sax for receptions, dinners and ceremonies.
  • DJs Sets that carry the night once the band finishes.
  • MCs Hosts who keep a programme moving and know when to stop talking.
  • Traditional performers Kenyan groups, including drummers and dancers.
  • Cultural acts Ensembles for heritage nights and diplomatic programmes.

The Process

Four Steps, No Surprises

  1. 01

    Tell us the brief

    Date, venue, audience and the feeling you are after. A rough budget helps us aim properly.

  2. 02

    We propose acts

    You get options with audio or video, and clear pricing against each one.

  3. 03

    We confirm and contract

    A single agreement covers the acts, the timings and the technical requirements.

  4. 04

    We run the night

    We handle load-in, sound check and the running order on the day.
